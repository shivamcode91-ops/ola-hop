# Ola Hop — clickable prototype

Book the seat, not the vehicle. Ola Hop finds the cheapest shared seat for your daily trip (e-rickshaw, shared auto, bus or car pool), books it before you arrive, takes UPI and shows where your ride is.

Live: https://shivamcode91-ops.github.io/ola-hop/

Story: Aarav plans Model Town → DTU, swaps any leg, picks his e-rickshaw seat, pays once for the whole trip. Sameer compares a Pune → Mumbai pool with a solo cab, picks a car and a seat. Plus a newcomer in Mumbai and a parent view.

Part of my Ola Electric APM product assignment (Oct 2026). Hop prices are illustrative.
